## Current Research

### Beyond Shared Geometry: Discovering Approximate Group Representations in Neural Network Activations

My current research studies whether semantic transformations can be represented by approximately compositional operators acting on LLM hidden states, and whether this structure generalizes across prompts, concepts, and layers beyond simple geometric baselines.

The project focuses on transformer hidden representations, activation-space geometry, equivariance, operator learning, composition, inverse consistency, and statistical validation of representation structure. The goal is to determine whether transformations of meaning correspond to structured geometric transformations inside neural networks, while testing the robustness of the observed structure against geometric baselines and alternative explanations.

*Ongoing research.*

### Robust Multi-Objective Challenger Search for Stable Gradient-Boosted Models

This research studies Less Discriminatory Alternative (LDA) model search as a robust multi-objective machine-learning problem, jointly considering predictive performance, fairness, sparsity, and stability. Here, LDA refers to Less Discriminatory Alternative rather than Linear Discriminant Analysis.

The work frames challenger search as robust ML and multi-objective model selection, combining XGBoost / gradient-boosted models, mixed-variable multi-objective optimization, NSGA-III, feature subset selection, hyperparameter optimization, fairness evaluation, model sparsity, OOS/OOT validation, threshold sensitivity, seed and hyperparameter sensitivity, and SHAP reason-code stability.

The broader methodological goal is to identify challenger models that improve fairness-related outcomes while remaining predictive, sparse, interpretable, and stable across perturbations and validation settings.

*Ongoing research.*
